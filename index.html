Why this exists

SMALL COMPANY. REAL WORK.

Sovereign Brutality is an independent extreme wrestling company starting in Rhode Island. We want to bring wrestling back into the community — halls, outdoor cards, nights people around here actually go to.

Wrestling is still a serious, dangerous job. It takes years, injuries, travel, and nights nobody sees. We respect that. Respect here does not mean we have a TV deal or a big payroll. It means we tell you the truth before you give us your Saturday.

01

Community shows

Local rooms. Local crowds. We want wrestling that belongs in Rhode Island, not a fantasy of a national takeover.

02

Honest pay, small pay

When we can pay, the starting number is about $20 a match. That is gas-and-a-meal money, not a living. If that is not enough, do not book us — we would rather lose a booking than lie.

03

Volunteers keep it alive

Refs, coaches, camera people, ring crew, merch, door. If you want to get paid, say so. If you want to donate time to get this off the ground, say that too. Both are welcome. Neither gets a bait-and-switch.

04

A network, not a corporation

This page is here so people can find us — wrestlers, officials, filmers, venues, and anyone who can help stand up a first card. We are looking for help, not investors who think this is the next WWE.

No sugarcoat

WHAT WE CAN ACTUALLY PAY

We believe people who wrestle, officiate, film, and run a show deserve money. We also know what a brand-new Rhode Island indie can afford on day one.

Current honest rate About $20 a match for in-ring talent when the door can support it. Same spirit for officials and crew if there is anything left after the room, insurance, and basic production. If the show cannot cover that, we say so before anyone drives in. Volunteers are how a first card happens. Paid spots come when the money is actually in the box, not when a website makes a promise.

If funding or a sponsor shows up later, pay goes up. Until then, assume small indie. Come because you want to build something in Rhode Island — not because you think this is a high-end contract.
