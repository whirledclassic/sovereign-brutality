HOUSE RULES

SAFETY & CONDUCT

This is how we treat each other. It applies to every wrestler, manager, commentator, referee, announcer, security guard, crew member, and anyone else working a Sovereign Brutality show.

The line

ZERO TOLERANCE. NO SECOND CHANCE.

Immediate cut Any form of sexual harassment, sexual assault, or harassment of anybody who works at our shows is an immediate cut. There are no warnings and no second chances. We take sexual assault and sexual harassment seriously.
01

Respect the people you work with

Locker rooms, hallways, ringside, and the parking lot are a workplace. Talk to people like coworkers. No slurs. No stalking. No threats. No “it was just a joke” when someone asks you to stop.

02

Sexual harassment is not booked

Unwanted comments about bodies or gear, sexual jokes aimed at a person, pressure for dates, touching without a clear yes, or using a work conversation to push a sexual angle — all of it is prohibited. In-ring spots that were agreed in advance are wrestling. Everything else needs consent.

03

Harassment of any worker is a cut

That includes talent, officials, commentary, security, crew, and venue staff. If you harass someone working the show — sexually or otherwise — you are done with this company the same night.

04

Safety in the room

Protect your opponent. Call off a spot that is going wrong. No cheap shots meant to injure. If someone is hurt, stop and get help. Family-friendly extreme still means real contact. It does not mean reckless.

If something happens

TELL THE PROMOTER

Report it to the promoter as soon as you can — the same night if you are safe to do so. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 first. A report will not be treated as a booking problem for the person who came forward.

Sovereign Brutality will remove the person who crossed the line. That decision is final.
