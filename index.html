The show

WHAT YOU WALK INTO

Sovereign Brutality is independent wrestling from Rhode Island. The night is built like a real card: music, lights, a bell, and matches that tell a story. Extreme means chairs and tables when the match calls for it — not glass, not fire, not a room kids cannot sit in.

The ring

HELP US OWN THE FLOOR

A delivered rental already quotes around a thousand dollars a night. Four or five of those nights buy a ring we keep. Donors who give a name may be printed on the first ring skirt. We print a first name or short nickname only. Offensive, vulgar, hateful, or otherwise inappropriate names will not be used. Anonymous gifts stay off the skirt. HOUSE MUSIC BY AMBERGRIS

We did not pick a random playlist. We picked a Rhode Island band we actually listen to.
