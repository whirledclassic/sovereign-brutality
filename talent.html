Roster advance — $100 Only if invited onto the official roster, and only if the budget can support it. Confirmed in writing or it does not exist.
In-ring pairing Men wrestle men. Women wrestle women. That is the house rule for matches only — not for managers, commentary, officials, security, or crew. A male wrestler may work with a female manager, and a female wrestler may work with a male manager. The split is strictly in-ring, and everyone on the show is treated with the same professional respect.
House conduct — zero tolerance Threats, harassment of workers, theft, sexual harassment, and sexual assault are an immediate cut. No second chances. Read the house rules.
