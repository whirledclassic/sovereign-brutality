The show

WHAT YOU WALK INTO

Sovereign Brutality is independent wrestling from Rhode Island. The night is built like a real card: music, lights, a bell, and matches that tell a story. Extreme means chairs and tables when the match calls for it — not glass, not fire, not a room kids cannot sit in.

The sound of the room

HOUSE MUSIC BY AMBERGRIS

Providence • Instrumental stoner / doom

AMBERGRIS

We did not pick a random playlist. We picked a Rhode Island band we actually listen to.

AMBERGRIS is thick, low, and heavy in the best way — dirty riffs that make a hall feel like a show before anyone has walked to the ring. That is why they are in this building. Their record is the house music on this site and the rumble in the room before the bell.

Put on Bottom Feeder from the new EP Untitled. If it grabs you the way it grabbed us, buy the music and the merch from them.
