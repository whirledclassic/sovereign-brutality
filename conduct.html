HOUSE RULES

HOW WE WORK

This is the standard for anyone on a Sovereign Brutality date — wrestlers, managers, commentary, referees, announcers, security, crew, and venue staff. Treat the building like a workplace. Treat the people in it like coworkers.

The card

WHAT GETS YOU CUT

Zero tolerance — no second chance Threats, harassment of anyone working the show, theft, violence outside a booked spot, sexual harassment, and sexual assault are an immediate cut. No warnings. No “we will talk about it next week.” You are done with this company the same night. That decision is final.
01

Respect the workers

Locker room, hallway, ringside, merch table, and parking lot are all part of the job. No slurs. No stalking. No putting hands on people who did not ask. If someone tells you to stop, you stop.

02

No threats

Do not threaten workers, officials, the promoter, venue staff, or fans. Not in person, not in a message, not “as a joke.” A threat is enough. You do not have to follow through for it to end your booking.

03

No harassment

Do not ride people. Do not corner them. Do not blow up their phone after they asked you to leave it. Harassment of anybody who works our shows — any kind — is a cut.

04

Do not steal

Gear, merch, cash, phones, coolers, venue property, another worker’s bag — if it is not yours, do not take it. Theft is an immediate cut and we will involve the police when it is warranted.

05

Safety in the match

Protect your opponent. Call off a spot that is going wrong. No cheap shots meant to injure. Family-friendly extreme is still real contact. It is not a license to hurt someone.

06

Sexual harassment and assault

Unwanted comments, pressure, touching without a clear yes, or any sexual assault — immediate cut. Booked in-ring spots that both people agreed to are wrestling. Everything else needs consent. We take this seriously.

If something happens

TELL THE PROMOTER

Tell the promoter as soon as you can — the same night if you are safe to do so. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 first. A report will not be treated as a problem for the person who came forward.

Sovereign Brutality will remove the person who crossed the line. There is no second chance on this list.
