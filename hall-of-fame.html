HALL OF FAME

THE NAMES ON THE BUILDING

This is not a museum of extreme wrestling. It is the names Sovereign Brutality is willing to put on the building.

Who belongs

EVERYONE WHO MADE THE NIGHT

Roster wrestlers. Independents. Talent from other companies. Referees, announcers, commentary, security, and crew. House music, film, and graphics. The hall that hosted the show. The sponsor who showed up. If they helped make a Sovereign Brutality night, and they work the way this house works, they are welcome.

The standard

HOW THIS HOUSE WORKS

Family-friendly extreme. Controlled. Chairs and tables when the match calls for it. Not glass. Not fire. Not wire. A name goes up for what they did in this room, written in public: who they are, what they did here, and the night if there was one. When someone comes from another company, that company is named with them.

The wall

ONE NAME AT A TIME

The Hall of Fame opens when the first name is written. Living first. No first class for show. A conduct cut comes off the wall.
