APPLY TO THE COMPANY

Fill this so we can put you on the wall if you are signed. No titles yet. You are applying as a roster member.

Compensation Standard night-of rate is approximately $20 per match when the door supports it. If a date cannot pay, we say so before you travel.
Roster advance — $100 In limited cases the company may offer a one-time $100 roster advance to talent invited onto the official roster. It is not a booking fee, not match pay, and not available on request. It depends on budget. Highly sought talent are the most likely to be offered one. Selecting yes means consider me if invited. It does not reserve $100. Confirmed in writing or it does not exist.
For everyone on the show Free water will be in a cooler at every Sovereign Brutality event for wrestlers, officials, and crew. That is company hospitality. Drink it.

Any song is allowed in the building. We will play the music you want for your walkout. If the track is copyrighted, clips we post (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, the site) may be muted, blocked, or claimed. That is a platform issue, not a ban on your theme. Live, it still plays.

Sovereign Brutality takes no cut of talent merch. Your sales are yours. We want the table there because it helps you, not because we profit from it. House tables are limited and go back on the truck at the end of the night. They rotate show to show so more people get a turn. Bringing your own table is the surest way to have a spot. Venue size still decides how many tables fit. If the room is tight we will say so early, split a table, or share a surface. We will try to get everyone on the floor. We will not promise a table the building cannot hold. Teamwork on this is part of working here.

The $100 advance is discretionary, budget-dependent, and only tied to an official roster invitation.

You hear back only if there is a next step.
